# v4 G3 balance report

200 AI-vs-AI matches, fixed seeds 1–200, the final 40-card deck (docs/v4-gameplay-update.md 5.4), measured
against section 6. Generated by `node v4/tools/balance.mjs`; nothing in the rules was changed. The AI is
a one-move greedy player, so the numbers describe AI play: Elliott's playtest has the final say.

## Suggestions (numbers to change)

- **Favours** share (11%, target 5%–10%): change `RULES.perFavour` from 3 to about **2**.
- **Objectives** share (3%, target 5–10%): raise `RULES.perObjective` (Recognition per Objective point) from 2 to **5**, which moves the share to about 8% (the share grows in step with it); or, keeping 2, shorten Misty's Objective from 8 defeats to 6 so more points are reached.
- **$ earned per player** (13.9 < ~15): raise `RULES.dollarsPerRound` from 1 to 2 (≈ +8 per match), or `dollarsPerDefeat` from 1 to 2.
- **Heat Wave** is played in only 22% of the matches where it is drawn (< 30%): lower its Adapt cost by 1 (the AI may also undervalue it; check in a playtest).
- **Quick Claw** is played in only 15% of the matches where it is drawn (< 30%): lower its price by $1 (the AI may also undervalue it; check in a playtest).
- **Bubble Beam React** ("the attacker becomes Spent after its attack") has no effect in v4: an attacker is always Spent after attacking. Suggestion: "the attack deals 2 less damage" (cost 1), or "the attacker stays Spent through the next Breakfast" (it doesn't become Active next Round).
