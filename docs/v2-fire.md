# v2 Fire deck, "Burn" (Aggressive)

**Status: approved by Elliott (3 Nov).** V6 builds it.

**V7 balance (Elliott):** every **Fire-Type** Pokémon has **+1 Strength at every Level** (V7.1: by Type, so Mankey, Sneasel, Duskull and Absol keep their original Strength; the Fire Partners and Entei have it). The numbers below include it.

## Identity in v2

- **Plan:** win the race for Recognition. Swift raiders hit the enemy player early. Burn on the enemy player strips their Recognition every Supper (v2's Burn rule), so Fire both **gains** and **takes away** Recognition.
- **Trade-off:** fragile Pokémon, few guards and little healing. It wants matches decided before the Championship.
- **Training plan:** mostly **light and standard** lines with low caps, so Fire has cheap, early bodies every Round. One heavy line (Magby) and the Legendary are the late punch.
- **Neutrals kept from N1:** Duskull → Dusclops (now a line), Absol, Hyper Voice, Black Belt.
