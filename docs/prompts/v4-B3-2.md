**A. Fixes and rulings from the B3-1 report** (Elliott, 13 November 2026):
1. **Accepted as written:** these readings in FOUNDATION section 16 stand. Mark them "Elliott, 13 Nov 2026".
   - On Dominate counts Tournament dominance.
   - Drain heals the damage dealt after Shell, only if the attacker survives.
   - Agility blocks only Moves.
   - Erika counts once per effect, and Rage doesn't count.
   - Whitney counts PokéMart purchases only.
   - Eevee doesn't count Normal.
2. **Sturdy resets when its card leaves play.** A card that goes back to hand (Dive, Swimmer, Escape Rope, Ralts, Gastly's line…) comes back fresh, Sturdy unused. This replaces the B3-1 reading. Add it to the `b3rules` test.
3. **Art Pending in the hand fan:** the "ART PENDING" words were cut off where hand cards overlap. In the fanned hand, and anywhere else only part of a card's art shows, hide the words and keep the glow. Full, hovered and Codex cards keep the words. Extend `cardtext.test.js` to cover it.

**B. New rules Batch 2 needs** (bible section 4 where it covers them; the rest settled here):
1. **Triggers:**
   - **On Command:** fires after you play a Move as Adapt (when it resolves) or as Strat (when it's sealed). Reacts don't trigger it.
   - **On Supper:** fires at Supper after Strats and before dominance, in lane order, active player first.
   - **Aura:** constant while in play. It stacks across different Pokémon but not with itself (two copies of the same Aura count once).
2. **Hand control:**
   - **"The opponent discards a random card":** the engine's seeded random choice.
   - **"The opponent discards a card you choose"** (Sabrina): the chooser sees the opponent's hand in a picker for that choice only. The AI may read the player's hand **only inside that choice**.
   - **"Put a random card from the opponent's hand into yours"** (Thief, Burglar): the card changes owner. A Pokémon card keeps its hand Level bonus.
   - **If the opponent's hand has fewer cards than asked,** take what there is.
3. **The Discard Pile:**
   - **"Summon a Pokémon of Level N or less from your Discard Pile, for free"** (Mr. Fuji, Revive):
     - the Pokémon's Level is this Round's Level for its series;
     - it goes into an empty space you choose, as a normal summon, so its On Summon fires.
   - **"Return a Pokémon card from your Discard Pile to your hand"** (Duskull line, Shadow Ball, Morty, Mismagius): the player picks from the Discard Pile in a picker; the AI picks with its own scoring.
4. **Going back to hand:**
   - **"Return another of your Pokémon to your hand with +N Levels"** (Kirlia, Gardevoir): uses B3-1's hand Level bonus.
   - **"On Defeated: return it to your hand"** (Gastly line):
     - it still counts as defeated (Recognition, $ and Objectives as normal);
     - the card goes to its owner's hand instead of the Discard Pile;
     - its Held Item returns to the PokéMart.
5. **Defeat effects:**
   - **"The Pokémon that defeated it"** (Mankey line, Destiny Bond's Adapt): if a Move or effect defeated it, there's no such Pokémon and nothing happens.
   - **Destiny Bond's React:** if this attack defeats your Pokémon, the attacker is defeated too. You get the normal defeat rewards for it.
6. **Moves and Items:**
   - **Close Combat:** pick one of your Pokémon. The enemy across from it takes the damage, and yours takes its damage. You can't pick a Pokémon with nothing across from it.
   - **Sucker Punch's Adapt:** targets only an Active enemy Pokémon.
   - **Detect's Adapt:** Retaliate 2 until Supper.
   - **Choice Specs:** Agility while held.
   - **X Speed:** Active, and Speed one step higher until Supper.
   - **Ether:** +2 Energy this Round.
7. **Supporters and Locations:**
   - **Bill:** the player sees their Draw Pile as a list sorted by name, never in order, picks 2, then the pile is shuffled.
   - **Officer Jenny's Favour:** "They also take 1 damage" means each Pokémon made Spent takes 1.
   - **Lavender Town:** when a Pokémon there is defeated, its owner draws 1 card.
8. **Objective counters** (tracked from setup):
   - **Sabrina:** Moves you play as Adapt or Strat.
   - **Bruno:** enemy attacks on your Pokémon that don't defeat it.
   - **Morty:** cards that leave your Discard Pile through your effects, returned to hand or summoned.
   - **Karen:** times your effects or attacks damage the enemy Player Character.
9. **Mewtwo:** cost 5, Level 8. On Command deals 2 damage to each enemy Pokémon in a Location you choose.
