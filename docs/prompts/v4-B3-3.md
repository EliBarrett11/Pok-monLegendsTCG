**A. Elliott's rulings on the B3-2 report** (13 November 2026):
1. **When neither player has a Pokémon left** in play, in hand or in their Draw Pile:
   - the match ends at that Round's Supper;
   - the player with more Recognition wins;
   - on a tie, the player whose Player Character has more Strength wins;
   - if that's tied too, it's a draw.

   Show it on the end screen as "Out of Pokémon". Add a test.
2. **No summon is ever free:**
   - **Route 119** now reads "Summoning a Pokémon here costs 1 less (minimum 1)." `cards.json` is already updated, so regenerate the data.
   - **Remove the AI's B3-2 rule** that it never re-summons a card returned to hand that Round. A Pokémon saved by Dive or returned by Ralts can be replayed the same Round, by either player.
   - **Keep one engine-level guard** so a turn can never loop forever. If the same full state repeats within a turn, the AI must pass. Test it.
3. **The five choices in the B3-2 report stand**, marked "Elliott, 13 Nov 2026" in FOUNDATION:
   - defeating your own Pokémon pays nobody;
   - Destiny Bond's React protects only a Pokémon;
   - Karen counts Overrun damage, and Bruno counts 0-damage attacks;
   - On Command doesn't fire on a fizzled Adapt;
   - cards are picked one at a time.

**B. New rules Batches 3–4 need** (bible section 4 where it covers them; the rest settled here):
1. **Shell:** Shell from different sources adds up. Iron Defense's kept Shell 1 on a Pokémon with Shell 1 gives Shell 2. Shell never goes below 0.
2. **Kept −Attack and −Strength** (Toxic, Sludge Bomb, Koffing line, Koga, Muk):
   - they last like kept gains;
   - Attack can't go below 0;
   - kept −Strength lowers the maximum, and a Pokémon whose Strength reaches 0 is defeated as normal.
3. **Speed changes:**
   - "Speed one step higher (kept)" (Dragon Dance) and "one step lower" (Seafoam Islands) move one step between Slow, Steady and Swift, and stop at the ends.
   - Tailwind's Adapt affects all your Pokémon until Supper.
4. **Board-wide and conditional effects:**
   - **"Each other enemy Pokémon"** (Rayquaza): every enemy except the target.
   - **Earthquake:** hits your Pokémon too, as written.
   - **Flash Cannon's "if one of your Pokémon holds an Item"** is checked when it resolves.
5. **Moves and Items:**
   - **Aerial Ace:** no React window opens for it.
   - **Assault Vest:** "Moves deal 1 less damage to it" (minimum 0).
   - **Life Orb:** the 1 damage happens after its attack resolves, and it can defeat the holder (nobody is paid, as in A3).
   - **Focus Sash:** gives Sturdy while held, and resets like Sturdy (B3-2).
6. **Locations:**
   - **Stark Mountain:** "At Supper, 1 damage to each Pokémon here" hits both sides, after On Supper triggers and before dominance.
   - **Dragon's Den** and **Viridian Forest**: their Level conditions use the Pokémon's current Level.
7. **Pokémon effects:**
   - **Auras with conditions** (Sewaddle line: "Level 1–3"; Geodude line: Strength) work like Riolu's: constant, and not stacking with a copy of themselves.
   - **Cubone line:** counts Pokémon cards in your Discard Pile when it's summoned, using the same pile B3-2 uses for "your Discard Pile".
   - **Swablu line:** "a Pokémon card in your hand gains +1 Level" uses B3-1's hand Level bonus. The player picks the card, and the AI picks with its own scoring.
8. **Supporters:**
   - **Bug Catcher:** "Level 3 or less" is the card's Level this Round. Use the picker from B3-2.
   - **Hiker** and **Ace Trainer** need nothing new.
9. **Objective counters** (tracked from setup):
   - **Lance:** Pokémon of Level 6 or more you summon.
   - **Jasmine:** times you attach a Held Item.
   - **Koga:** effects of yours that lower an enemy Pokémon's Attack. Each effect counts once, however many Pokémon it touches.
10. **Rayquaza:** cost 5, Level 8, as the other Legendaries.

**Where a rule still isn't settled,** leave that card out of the decks and list the card and the question in the report. Don't invent a rule.
