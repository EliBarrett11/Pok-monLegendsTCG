**v4 C1: the card renderer, with the new card designs used everywhere a card appears**

Work only in `v4/` (plus the shared `assets/`, read only). First read `docs/v4-card-overhaul.md` sections 1, 2 and 6, approved by Elliott on 10 November 2026. Then open the approved mockup, `docs/design/v4-cards-mockup.jpg`.

**Copy the mockup's source:** take the SVG frame paths, sizes, colours, fonts, text zones, stat tabs, cost wells and type lines from `docs/design/v4-cards-mockup.html`. Match the mockup closely. If the game needs something the mockup doesn't show, design it in the same style and list it in your report.

**Scope:**
- No rule, number or card changes.
- The engine stays DOM-free.
- This stage is the renderer only: per-card focal points, the holo and tilt finish, and the two new pieces of art come in C2; the Codex comes in C3.

**A. `src/ui/card.js`: one renderer for every card.**
- **`renderCard(card, { size, state })`** returns the card's element.
- **Sizes:**
  - **`full`:** 340×476, for hover zoom, the React window, the mode picker, the Release choice, and the Discard and Released viewers.
  - **`mini`:** 200 wide, for the hand and the PokéMart card.
  - **`token`:** 170 wide, for a Pokémon on the board.
  - **`chip`:** a 24–40 px round art crop, for trackers, the Bag and the log.
- **Drawing:** cards are drawn at 340×476 and scaled, as in the mockup.
- **State:** playable / unplayable with its reason, Ready, Spent, damaged, selected, legal target, illegal target with its reason, Retained, Sold out.
- **Frames:** one per card type, exactly as section 2.2 and the mockup:
  - **Pokémon:** chamfered top corners.
  - **Command:** a notch in the top edge that seats the cost.
  - **Supporter:** a low arch along the top.
  - **Item:** an eyelet.
  - **Trainer:** a central peak.
  - **Location:** scroll rolls.

  Each has a dark edge, a brass hairline and an inner rule. Colour is only on accents.
- **What each size shows:**
  - **Full:** the cost well, the crest, the panel rule with its accent diamond, the name, the type line, then:
    - a keyword line and the rules;
    - or the Command mode rows (only the modes `commandModesFor` allows now, the others dimmed);
    - or the Supporter's Favour box;
    - or the Item's Stock diamonds;
    - or the Trainer's Objective dots and Partner portrait;
    - or the Location's chips.

    Pokémon also get the two flush stat tabs, with Level and Speed between them.
  - **Mini:** art, cost, name and stat tabs, plus **one tag**: the first trigger ("On Summon"), or the available Command modes as chips ("ADAPT 2 · STRAT 3").
  - **Token:** the Pokémon frame with its art, a keyword chip, the Held Item as a round inset, the name, the stat tabs, Level and Speed.
- **The art:** fills the card (`preserveAspectRatio xMidYMid slice`), with a default focal point of 50% across, 35% down. C2 replaces this with per-card focal points, so read it from a single function, `artFocus(cardId)`.
- **The Partner** shows ★ in place of its Level.

**B. Neat text, guaranteed** (section 2.2a):
- **Zones:** every piece of text sits in its frame's fixed zone. Nothing overlaps another element, and nothing is cut off.
- **Names:** one line. A long name may use one tighter letter-spacing step, never a smaller font.
- **Rules text:** 14.5 px on Full cards. **Write each card's on-card wording to fit its zone.** Where today's text doesn't fit, shorten the wording on the card (the full text stays in the data for the Codex) and list every shortened card in your report, before → after, for Elliott to check.
- **Numbers:** they fit two digits in their tabs and wells.
- **The test: `node v4/tests/cardtext.test.js`** (Playwright is allowed) renders **every card in the game** at every size, including Partners, Trainers, Locations and the PokéMart pool. A Pokémon is rendered at every Level, a Command in every mode state, and a Pokémon with and without a Held Item and keyword. It fails when any text element:
  - overflows its box (`scrollWidth` / `scrollHeight`);
  - overlaps another text element or a stat tab, cost well or crest (bounding-box intersection);
  - extends outside the card's frame;
  - renders a font size below 10 px on screen at its size.
