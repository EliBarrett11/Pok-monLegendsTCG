**A. Pass (2.1).**
- **The rule:** a player can't Pass while they have a Ready Pokémon with unmatched Speed, meaning the opponent has no Active Pokémon of the same Speed.
- **The exception:** if every such Pokémon has no legal target, Pass is allowed, and they become Spent, as today.
- **The interface:** End turn is disabled, with the reason, e.g. "Charizard is Ready and faster than anything the Opponent has: attack first". Space does nothing then.
- **The AI** must follow the rule through `legalActions`.
- **The end-turn confirmation from UX3** must not offer a Pass that isn't legal.

**B. React (2.2).**
- **A React window opens only for:**
  - an attack by a Pokémon;
  - a Command played as Adapt, when it's played;
  - a Command played as Strat, when it resolves, at the start of its owner's next turn or at Supper.
- **No React window for** summons, playing a Strat face-down, or Trainer Commands.
- **A Strat that resolves at Supper** must still give the other player their React window there, before dominance. The interface's React window (banner, timer, keys) must work for it too.
- **A React that removes the Strat's target** makes the Strat fizzle, as the existing fizzle rules say.

**C. The cost curve (2.3).** `costFor(level) = Math.ceil(level / 2)`. Partners stay cost 4. Update the comment in `rules.js`.

**D. Recognition (2.4):** dominating a Location +3, a Favour +3, dominating the Tournament +5. Damage, defeats and Objectives are unchanged.

**E. PokéDollars (2.5):** +$1 at each Breakfast (unchanged), and +$1 flat for each enemy Pokémon you defeat, Partners included, replacing "the defeated Pokémon's cost".

**F. The PokéMart (2.6):**
- **One slot.** The current six Items get these prices and Stock; G3 adds the rest:

  | Item | Price | Stock |
  |---|---|---|
  | Oran Berry | $1 | 8 |
  | Potion | $2 | 6 |
  | Choice Band | $2 | 6 |
  | Charcoal | $2 | 6 |
  | Mystic Water | $2 | 6 |

  Store Stock on each Item's definition as `stock`.
- **Stock:** each turn taken by either player in the Battle Phase uses 1 Stock (React decisions aren't turns). At 0, the Item goes back to the pool and a random Item from the pool replaces it, with full Stock. Stock carries over between Rounds; remove the refresh at Breakfast.
- **Buying it:** the slot shows **Sold out** until the buyer has finished their next turn. Then a random Item arrives with full Stock. Nothing counts down while it's sold out.
- **The pool:** the pool, deck copies returning to it, buying, and the Bag work as today.

- **The AI** values the PokéMart Item as before, and knows a bought Item takes it away from the opponent.

**G. Trainer Objective point moments (2.7)**, a new presenter beat for the `objectiveProgress` event, inspired by Legends of Runeterra's Champion level-up track. For either player:

1. **The portrait:** a dark backdrop fades in over the board, and the Trainer's portrait slides in large from that player's side, with the Trainer's name and the Objective text.
2. **The track:** a track of pips sits below the portrait, one per point needed, with the Partner's portrait at its end. The new pip stamps in with a flash and a rising "+1", and the count ticks over ("3 → 4 / 8"). The Partner's portrait glows a step brighter with each point.
3. **The return:** the portrait slides back into the tracker, which pulses.
4. **Timing:** about 1.2 s at Normal. Skippable with a click; follows the speed setting; Low motion keeps the stamp and count but drops the movement.
5. **The final point:** a longer version where the track fills, the portrait flares gold, and it leads straight into the existing Partner arrival beat.

Several points in one action (e.g. two defeats) play as one moment with the count ticking twice.
