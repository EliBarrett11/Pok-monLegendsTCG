**A. Triggers (4.1)**, as data on each Pokémon form, resolved by the engine:
- **The six triggers:**
  - **On Summon:** when it arrives, including a Partner.
  - **On Attack:** after it attacks, if it survived.
  - **On Defend:** when it survives an attack that damaged it.
  - **On Defeated:** when it's defeated.
  - **On Knockout:** when its attack, or its hit-back, defeats an enemy Pokémon.
  - **On Breakfast:** at each Breakfast while in play.
- **Effects:** a small set of effects, each with an amount:
  - draw cards;
  - +N Energy this turn;
  - damage to a **chosen** enemy Pokémon (On Summon only; the player picks with the UX3 targeting flow, and the AI picks through its normal choice);
  - damage to each enemy Pokémon;
  - damage to each enemy Pokémon in its own Location, or in the target's Location (optionally excluding the target);
  - heal one of your Pokémon (chosen), each of your Pokémon in its Location (optionally excluding itself), or itself;
  - +N Attack until Supper to your other Pokémon in its Location;
  - +N Attack or +N Strength (kept) to itself, or to a chosen other Pokémon of yours in its Location.

  A chosen target with no legal choice makes that part fizzle.
- **"In its Location":** for a Pokémon in a lane with no Location, it means just its own lane.
- **Order:** triggers resolve after the action that caused them, in a fixed, documented order. Write it in `FOUNDATION_ASSUMPTIONS.md`, e.g. attacker's On Attack / On Knockout, then defender's On Defend / On Defeated.
- **Chains:** a trigger can cause further triggers (a damage trigger defeating something fires On Defeated). Cap the depth so it can't loop.
- **React:** triggers are not Command Cards, so they can't be Reacted to.

**B. Keywords (4.1):**
- **Quick Attack:** arrives Active instead of Spent.
- **Infiltrator:** its attacks ignore guards (Pokémon and Player Character).
- **Overrun:** when its attack defeats an enemy Pokémon, the leftover damage hits the enemy Player Character as Recognition for you, at the normal rate.
- **Shell X:** each instance of damage it takes is X less, minimum 1.
- **Regenerate X:** heals X at Breakfast.
- **Retaliate X:** when an attack damages it, the attacker takes X.
- **Rage:** +1 Attack for good each time it survives damage.

They stack with Items, Locations and Weather. Shell applies to Retaliate damage too, as in v2.
