**Scope:**

**A. The Codex panel:**
- **Opening it:**
  - **right-click** anything when no choice is in progress (right-click still cancels a choice in progress);
  - **C** on whatever is under the pointer;
  - a small **ⓘ** on every hovered Full card.
- **Where it sits:** over the right-hand column and part of the board, leaving your hand and the board's centre visible. The match carries on underneath, and the AI waits while it's open on your turn. **Esc** or **✕** closes it.
- **Layout, card first:**
  - **On the left:** the **Full card at 340 px** (with its holo and tilt), or for things that aren't cards, their own header.
  - **On the right**, in this order:
    1. **What it does now:** the card's full rules text (the Codex is where full wording lives, including anything shortened on the card), plus its current state.
    2. **Where its numbers come from:** Attack, Strength, cost and Speed, each as a short sum, e.g. "Attack 9 = 8 base + 1 Mt. Ember". Build these from the engine's own functions, as the UX3 previews do, so they always match.
    3. **Keywords and triggers:** each with its glossary meaning.
    4. **For a Pokémon:**
       - its **forms by Level** as a row of small Mini cards (e.g. Charmander 3–4, Charmeleon 5–6, Charizard 7–8), with the current one marked;
       - for a card in hand, **what it becomes next Round** ("Lv 5 next Round: Attack 4, Strength 6"), using v2's ▲/▼ version arrows.
    5. **Links:** every game term in all of the above is a link to its glossary entry, with **Back** and breadcrumbs.

**B. What can be inspected** (UX5 A, updated for G1–G3 and C1–C2):
- **Any card:** in the hand, on the board, in the PokéMart, the Bag, the Discard, the Released viewer, the React window, the mode picker or the log.
- **A Location:** its passive, lanes, Rounds left, the Weather it adds, Strength on each side, and its dominance reward (+3).
- **A Trainer Objective tracker:** the goal, progress, and the Partner's Full card.
- **A Recognition ring:** history by source (damage, defeats, Overrun, dominance, Favours, Objectives, Amulet Coin, Tournament bonus), with the Round of each.
- **The Energy tile:** this Round's Base Energy, the Pass bonus, Exert and Exhaust, and Chinchou's bonus if any.
- **The Weather chip:** the full bracket table, the current totals and which Locations add them.
- **The PokéMart:** the Item, its Stock and how Stock counts down, Sold out and when it restocks.
- **The piles:** the Deck as a count only, never its order. The Discard and Released piles with their contents.
- **A log line,** the Tournament, a glossary term, and **Rules**.
