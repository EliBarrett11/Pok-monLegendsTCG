**A. Remove Weather completely.** Elliott removed it (12 November 2026).

- **What stays:** Locations keep their passive, lanes, Rounds and dominance.

**B. Cards come from data.**
1. **A tool generates the data:** `v4/tools/buildcards.mjs` reads `docs/base-game/cards.json` and writes `v4/src/data/cards.js`, a plain ES module with no build step at runtime. **Commit the generated file.** Re-running the tool on unchanged input gives an identical file.
2. **Every effect is structured.** Each trigger, Move mode, Supporter, Item, Location passive, Trainer Command and Objective becomes ops from **one effect vocabulary**. Extend the existing ops: `damage`, `heal`, `draw`, `energy`, `tempAttack`, kept `attack`/`strength`, and the existing targets. Add only what Batch 1 needs. Document the vocabulary at the top of the data module, as `cards.js` does now. The cards' wording is matched to the vocabulary inside `buildcards.mjs`, not hand-typed in the game.
3. **The wording is generated, and it must match exactly.**
   - Card text, Codex text and Move mode lines are generated from the ops.
   - **`node v4/tests/cardsdata.test.js`** checks that the generated wording equals `cards.json`, word for word, for every Batch 1 entry: `text`, `cardText` and the favour `text`.
4. **Pokémon series:** use `forms`, `stats` by Level, `speed` per form (Charizard and Kingdra are Swift), `guard`, and `keywords` per form. Cost is `ceil(Level / 2)`, and **5 for Legendaries**.
5. **The old card definitions are gone.** Everything the game uses now comes from the data module, including Misty, Blaine, Starmie and Ninetales, re-tuned as in `cards.json` (Misty's Command now costs 2, Blaine's 1).

**C. New rules Batch 1 needs.** Follow design bible section 4 exactly.
- **Trigger:** On Dominate.
- **Keywords:** Drain, Sturdy, Agility, Intimidate X, Stealth and Pickup.
- **Effects:**
  - "The attack deals no damage" (Protect's React).
  - "Shell X until Supper".
  - "becomes Spent", for one enemy and for each enemy in a Location.
  - "becomes Active".
  - "Speed one step higher (kept)" and "until Supper".
  - "Return one of your Pokémon to your hand" (Swimmer).
  - **Levels for Pokémon cards in hand:**
    - "A Pokémon card in your hand gains +N Levels" and "Each Pokémon card in your hand gains +N Levels" (Fisherman, Gardenia, Celebi).
    - The bonus stays with that card while it's in hand and adds to its normal growth each Round.
    - A card still can't go past its series' top Level.
  - **Held Items that give a Type a keyword** (Charcoal, Mystic Water, Miracle Seed, Magnet): the keyword applies only while the holder is that Type.
  - "Gain N Recognition" (Amulet Coin).
  - Damage to the enemy Player Character from a Pokémon's trigger (Roselia, Zapdos).
- **Three Objective counters**, tracked from setup like Misty's and Blaine's:
  - **Erika:** times your Pokémon gain kept Attack or Strength. Each effect counts once, however many Pokémon it touches.
  - **Lt. Surge:** extra Energy gained from cards.
  - **Whitney:** Items bought.
- **Legendaries:** cost 5 and Level 8 from the start. They don't grow.

**Where a rule isn't settled,** leave that card out of the decks, and list the card and the question in the report. Don't invent a rule.

**E. Cards on screen.** Use the approved card design (C1–C4).
- **Art Pending:** a card with `"hasArt": false` shows an **Art Pending** panel in place of its art:
  - a soft radial glow in the card's Type or card-kind colour;
  - the words ART PENDING in small spaced capitals.

  It's drawn in code, not an image file. Elliott's rule: never wait for art.
- **New Type colours and crests:** Grass needs them. Moves show their Type's crest and colour (Normal Moves use Normal).
- **Locations** no longer show any Weather chip.
