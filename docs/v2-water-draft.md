# DRAFT: v2 Water deck, "Value-Midrange" (Board-Based)

**Status: a proposal for Elliott. Don't build from this file.** Same format as `docs/v2-fire.md`.

## Identity in v2

- **Plan:** hold the board and win the long game. Sturdy guards, healing and Rally make Water's Pokémon hard to remove, so it holds Locations and banks their milestones. It's the deck that most wants to reach **the Championship** with a board still standing.
- **Recognition comes from:** Location milestones and completions, and late finishers (Gyarados, Dragonite, Hydro Pump, Kyogre).
- **Trade-off:** slow, little early pressure on the enemy player. Fire wants to race it before Round 10.
- **Training plan:** mostly standard lines with high caps (Water likes its late forms), plus light early bodies (Wingull, Wooper, Chinchou), so every Round has a cheap play.
- **Rain** (Weather) boosts healing (+1) and dampens Burn: Water's Locations and Pelipper/Kyogre are a natural shield against Fire.
- **Neutrals from N1:** Dragonite (now Dratini → Dragonair → Dragonite), Thunderbolt, Rocky Helmet and Tracey stay. **Shinx is dropped:** in v2 every card is playable early as its Level-1 form, so Water no longer needs a cheap neutral body (Wingull fills the role).
- **No useless cards (Elliott):** even the weakest stages do something small. Magikarp draws a card; Happiny heals.
