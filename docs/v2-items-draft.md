# DRAFT: v2 shared Item pool

**Status: a proposal for Elliott.** The pool every player draws from when a Location rewards "find an Item" (v2-design 7a). One copy of each; used Items return to the pool. Each deck's own Item cards (in `v2-fire.md`, `v2-water.md`, `v2-grass.md`) are separate.

**Design aim:** generic, useful to any deck, never the best card in a deck. Found Items are a small bonus for working on Locations, so none should swing a match by itself.

| Item | Type | Effect | Art |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Potion** | Consumed | Heal a friendly Pokémon 3 | ✅ potion |
| **Super Potion** | Consumed | Heal a friendly Pokémon 5 and Douse it | ❌ super-potion |
| **Full Heal** | Consumed | Cure a friendly Pokémon, or your player character, of all statuses | ✅ full-heal |
| **Revive** | Consumed | Return a Pokémon from your Discard Pile to your hand; it gains Retain | ❌ revive |
| **X Attack** | Consumed | A friendly Pokémon gains +2 Attack this Round | ✅ x-attack |
| **Ether** | Consumed | +1 Energy now | ❌ ether |
| **Escape Rope** | Consumed | Move a friendly Pokémon to an empty space of yours (another lane or Location) | ❌ escape-rope |
| **Exp. Share** | Consumed | Gain 1 Trainer Point | ❌ exp-share |
| **Amulet Coin** | Consumed | Gain 2 Recognition | ✅ amulet-coin |
| **Quick Claw** | Held | Holder's Speed is one tier higher | ✅ quick-claw |
| **Leftovers** | Held | Holder gains Regenerate 1 | ✅ leftovers |
| **Rocky Helmet** | Held | Holder gains Retaliate 2 | ✅ rocky-helmet |

12 Items. **Art needed (5):** super-potion, revive, ether, escape-rope, exp-share.

**Note:** Quick Claw, Leftovers, Rocky Helmet and Full Heal are also deck cards (Fire, Water). A deck copy and a pool copy can both be in play.
