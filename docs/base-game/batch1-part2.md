## Notes for B3 (engine) and B5 (balance)

- **New Objective counters:** Erika counts kept Attack or Strength gains on your Pokémon; Lt. Surge counts extra Energy gained from cards (On Summon, On Breakfast, Commands); Whitney counts Items bought. Their targets (10, 8, 5) are first guesses for Round 5–7; B5's simulations set them, as they did for Misty and Blaine.
- **New effects:** "no damage" and "Shell X until Supper" (Protect, Wallace), Pokémon cards in hand gaining Levels (Gardenia, Fisherman, as Celebi), "each enemy in a Location becomes Spent" (Thunder Wave), Held Items that add a keyword for one Type (Charcoal, Mystic Water, Miracle Seed, Magnet).
- **Bigger changes to playtested cards:** Surf's Adapt now deals 3 to each enemy in a Location (was 2), and Heat Wave is cheaper but split into smaller hits. X Attack is $1 and makes the Pokémon Active; Amulet Coin gives 5 Recognition for $3 (was 3 for $4); Leftovers is Regenerate 2 for $2.
